Martini's 5 Core Commands

Martini's Husky Cheat Sheet

1. Pay High Wages

Huskies ask "What's in it for me?" Dry kibble won't compete with city pigeons or squirrels. Use tiny cubes of cheddar, boiled chicken, or frankfurter slices.

2. Short & Sweet (5–10 Mins)

Adult Huskies bore easily with repetitive drills. Stop while Martini is still enthusiastic and wanting more—two 5-minute sessions beat one 20-minute slog.

3. Train Before Meals

Practise right before breakfast or dinner when Martini is naturally food-motivated.

4. Say the Cue Once

Repeating "Martini, Martini, Martini" teaches him to tune you out. Say his name or cue once clearly, then help him succeed.

5. Generalise Gradually

Dogs don't generalise well. Master every command Indoors first, then in a Hallway / Garden, before expecting reliability in a busy City / Park.
